# Product Map

## Recommended Website Framing

The website should frame MyAI as a modular AI application suite powered by one shared Core, not as a disconnected list of separate products.

## Product Categories

### Foundation

- Core
- Studio
- UI / Craft

### Collaboration

- Council

### Development

- AIDE
- AIR Runtime (delivered through AIDE first, then reusable pattern)

### Knowledge

- KnowLedger

### Guided Execution

- GeneralProcedureScript / Illuminate

### Creative Experiences

- IBA

## Positioning Model

- Foundation products define the platform and experience system.
- Collaboration products support shared human and AI work, especially around rooms, meetings, tasks, and decisions.
- Development products support software creation and technical reasoning.
- AIR capabilities provide governed agent/tool orchestration for development operations.
- Knowledge products turn messy thought into durable reference material.
- Guided execution products turn assistance into active completion and reusable SOPs.
- Creative experience products support narrative and visual interaction modes.

## Governance and Ownership Alignment

- Default posture is deny-by-default for app access until explicitly granted by org policy.
- Studio is the policy surface for org defaults: visibility, uploader rights, restricted/private creation, and team/group sharing.
- Core enforces org-scoped context for access-sensitive operations.
- Council, KnowLedger, and MyAIDE consume the same policy decisions through shared contracts.
- Council should be treated as both a collaboration surface and a producer of reusable platform knowledge.
- Agents operate under the same principal/role model as users and remain fully auditable.
- AIR events and diff workflows must remain observable and approval-aware by default.

## Council-Specific Framing

- Council is the collaboration application in the suite.
- It should be presented as the place where users and personas work together in shared rooms.
- It bridges conversation and execution by turning room activity into tasks, decisions, summaries, and reusable knowledge.
- It is also a natural surface for meeting preparation, discussion, and follow-up as calendar and task workflows expand.
- Council-generated and Council-uploaded knowledge should be available to other MyAI experiences through shared Core contracts rather than staying isolated inside one app.

## Naming Notes

Current naming split:

- `GeneralProcedureScript`: the application or workflow engine.
- `Illuminate`: the guided walkthrough experience mode.

Possible marketable naming split:

- `MyAI Navigator`: app name.
- `Illuminate`: experience mode.

Working interpretation for now:

- GeneralProcedureScript is the guided execution application.
- Illuminate is the experience mode that turns questions into structured walkthroughs.
