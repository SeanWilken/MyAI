# MyAI Council

## One-Line Definition

The collaborative workspace for humans and AI personas.

## Positioning

Council lets users, teams, and AI personas collaborate in shared rooms for planning, execution, synthesis, decision-making, and follow-through across conversations, tasks, meetings, and documents.

## Responsibilities

- support direct and group collaboration
- enable persona participation in shared workspaces
- connect conversations to tasks, meeting workflows, and reusable artifacts
- capture outcomes as recaps, tasks, decisions, and knowledge assets
- contribute federated knowledge that can be reused across the MyAI suite

## Features

- direct user chats
- 1:n persona conversations
- user/persona group rooms
- department or team-scoped collaboration
- council-style multi-persona discussions
- tool-assisted workflows
- organization-scoped room creation with users and personas
- meeting, task, and document collaboration surfaces
- room-linked task creation and follow-up coordination
- reusable document sections and knowledge-backed uploads
- federated knowledge handoff into broader suite experiences
- shared recaps, summaries, and action packets

## Ecosystem Fit

- Core provides auth, org context, channels, tasks, personas, and knowledge contracts.
- Studio governs who can create rooms, attach personas, manage visibility, and access collaboration capabilities.
- Council is the shared execution and coordination surface where people and personas actually work together.
- KnowLedger and other knowledge-aware experiences can consume the artifacts and knowledge created in Council.
- Documents, notes, and uploaded sections created in Council should become reusable knowledge rather than app-local attachments.

## Cross-App Value

- A meeting discussion in Council can produce tasks and reusable summaries.
- A document section uploaded in Council can become federated knowledge for later retrieval in other apps.
- A task created in Council can stay linked to the room where it was discussed.
- Personas configured in Studio can participate in Council with org-aware policy and approval controls.

## Strategic Role

Council is the collaborative operating environment in MyAI for teams that want more than a private chatbot: a place where humans and personas coordinate around work, produce durable outcomes, and feed shared knowledge back into the platform.
